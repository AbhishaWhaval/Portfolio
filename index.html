04 — Research

Published papers

June 2026 Latest

Agentic AI for Autonomous Data Analytics: A Theoretical Framework with Visualization-Driven Analysis

International Journal of Innovative Research in Technology

Proposed ADAS — a five-module theoretical framework enabling an AI system to autonomously execute the complete data analytics lifecycle from natural language goal interpretation to finished report generation. Designed interdependent modules: Goal Interpreter, Hierarchical Planning Engine, Tool-Use Controller, Reasoning & Validation Layer, and Autonomous Reporting Engine. Developed a formal confidence scoring mechanism C(oᵢ) = αVᵢ + βRᵢ + γHᵢ + δKᵢ and a three-gate validation framework, grounded in LLMs, RAG, and multi-agent orchestration.
